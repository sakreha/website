What is intelligence made of? What is experience made of? What is the universe made of? What is civilization made of?

Sakreha Research is an independent research practice working across four connected fields of inquiry.

IAI & Intelligence

Can intelligence be modelled more efficiently — and can machine intelligence ever touch experience?

A search for alternatives to energy-guzzling transformer architectures, alongside a study of whether AI can meaningfully model, simulate, evoke, or possess qualia and free will.

IIConsciousness & Qualia

What does it take to articulate experience?

The hard problem of consciousness, examined alongside the nature of qualia, selfhood, agency, attention, embodiment, and free will.

IIIQFT, GR & the Structure of Reality

What is the universe made of at the deepest level?

A grounding in quantum field theory and general relativity as they stand today, toward an epistemological restructuring of physics — not a new theory of everything, but a new framework capable of explaining current observations from first principles.

IVCivilizations, Theogony, Art & Literature

How does human consciousness become civilization?

The origins and evolution of civilizations — how hegemonies form, how gods are created and absorbed, and how music, dance, literature, and ritual shape collective consciousness.
